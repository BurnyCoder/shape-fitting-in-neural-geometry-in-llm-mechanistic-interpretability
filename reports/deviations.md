Every change relative to [preregistration.md](preregistration.md) is listed here with its date and reason, as the preregistration requires. Analyses that were not preregistered are labelled **exploratory** here and everywhere else they appear.

### Found in the independent audit of 2026-11-14

None of these changes a preregistered verdict. Every number they refer to is in `results/main_s0/analysis.json` under the key named in the row. Items marked **post hoc** are exploratory.

| Date | Item | Preregistered | What was done | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-14 | Geometric AIC can never select the blob | B1 and the A5 G-AIC rule select with Kanatani's geometric AIC | The noise variance is estimated from the best non-volumetric candidate g, ε² = J_g / ((3 − d_g)N − p_g). Substituting it gives AIC_G(blob) − AIC_G(g) = J_blob + J_g + 2·p_blob·ε² > 0 for every cloud, so B1 and A5-G-AIC never output blob (`posthoc.blob_recall`, `posthoc.blob_predicted`). A unit test documents the property (`tests/test_infer_eval.py`). The preregistered B1 results are kept. **Post hoc:** H1/H2 are also reported on the clouds whose true family is not blob (`posthoc.nonblob`) and against B1-fixed (rows below). | A structural property of the implementation that had not been logged. It inflates the H1 effect size and the share of the A5 accuracy drop attributed to model selection. |
| 2026-11-14 | Clustered test set: bootstrap and Wilcoxon | "Resample seeds, then test samples within each seed"; Wilcoxon signed-rank on per-sample Chamfer | Test clouds are generated in chunks of 25 that share N and the R^D-embedding draw (`data/testsets.py`, `data/synth.py`). Clouds of one chunk are therefore not independent, and the baseline subset is 156 chunks of 20. Every synthetic CI is now a cluster bootstrap over chunks (2000 replicates). The Wilcoxon tests of H2 and H5b use per-chunk mean differences. With i.i.d. resampling (analysis of commit 476cc10) the CIs were narrower: H1 [0.357, 0.391], H2 [−0.0645, −0.0462], H5b [−0.0046, −0.0021]. | When samples are clustered, the independent units are the clusters, so they are what must be resampled (cluster bootstrap: Davison & Hinkley 1997, Sec. 3.8; [Field & Welsh 2007](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2007.00593.x)). |
| 2026-11-14 | "Unambiguous" subset depends on B1 | "Samples where a simpler family also fits are tagged ambiguous" | The tag comes from B1's own per-family fits. A cloud whose true family B1 fits badly is therefore tagged ambiguous; this happens for most helix clouds. The table is kept, now labelled "judged by B1's own fits". It conditions on B1's fitting success, which favours B1. | Interpretation. |
| 2026-11-14 | **Post hoc** H3 reference numbers | Coarse agreement against a label-permutation null | Two numbers are reported next to H3: the agreement of a constant predictor of the most frequent expected coarse label (`H3.constant_majority_agree_posthoc`), and agreement at the level of the exact family (`H3.fine_agree_subsample_posthoc`, `H3.fine_agree_full_cloud_posthoc`). The paper's "Successes" paragraph, which listed coarse agreements that are fine-level misses, is renamed "Coarse agreements". | The preregistration listed no trivial baseline for H3. |
| 2026-11-14 | H4 verdict wording | "C2 is labelled blob in ≥ 80% of subsamples" | The verdict now gives both readings: the implemented mean over datasets, and the number of preregistered datasets that reach 80% (`H4.C2_prereg_at_least_80_posthoc` of `H4.C2_prereg_n_posthoc`). | A flat "supported" hid that the per-dataset reading fails for the smallest clouds. |
| 2026-11-14 | **Post hoc: B1-fixed, pre-specified before it ran on the test set** | — | B1-fixed is B1 with three repairs and the same budget (4 starts, 300 refinement steps). (1) **Cluster candidates per cloud:** every k ≤ the cloud's own N. (2) **Exact J:** orthogonal point-to-shape distance by damped Gauss–Newton point inversion from the nearest dense sample ([Ma & Hewitt 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-8396(03)00021-9); [Hu & Wallner 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cagd.2004.12.001)), never larger than the grid distance. (3) **Blob null test:** a cloud is a blob if the RMS residual of its best surface fit, ε̂ = sqrt(J_g / N), exceeds τ; otherwise the non-blob candidate with the lowest G-AIC wins. τ is the ε̂ quantile (200-point grid) that maximises macro-F1 on 5 clouds per family and cell of the `val` stream (780 clouds), with ties going to the smallest τ. τ and its curve are saved in `results/main_s0/synth/b1_fixed_tau.json`. B1-fixed runs on the same 3,120 test clouds (`run.py tune-b1-fixed eval-b1-fixed`). The preregistered B1 stays the primary comparator for H1 and H2; method vs B1-fixed (`posthoc.method_vs_B1_fixed`) is reported whatever its direction. The real-data B1 column stays the preregistered B1. | To measure the H1/H2 effect sizes against a classical baseline without the three known handicaps. |
| 2026-11-14 | B1-fixed blob statistic changed after a smoke run | — | The first version thresholded Kanatani's dof-corrected ε̂ = sqrt(J_g / ((3 − d)N − p)). A smoke run (50 steps, 2 starts) used 156 smoke-sized clouds from the `test` stream, generated with a different chunking from the main test set, and 312 `val` clouds. For N ≤ p the clamped degrees of freedom made ε̂ explode: 5-point lines got ε̂ ≈ 0.8, and most clouds with 5–12 points were called blob. The statistic was replaced by the RMS residual before any full B1-fixed run. | A mathematical defect visible in the smoke run. It is disclosed because the smoke clouds came from the test stream. |
